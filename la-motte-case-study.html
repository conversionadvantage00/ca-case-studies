the brief

grow La Motte’s online wine sales, and get the estate found in the places people now go to ask for recommendations.

organic visibility usually takes years to mature. La Motte needed results from its online shop now, plus a way to show up in AI-powered chat discovery as well as on Google.

we ran the full digital retainer across social, search and the website, then added a mobile-first microsite with a stronger focus on sales-driven conversion paths and newsletter growth.

01 · full digital retainer6 months

revenue more than doubled.

social offers drove shoppers to La Motte’s online store, with wine specials such as “buy 6 bottles and get 10% off” on the 2018 Syrah and the Pierneef Syrah Viognier.

at the same time, search did a growing share of the work. 58% of online sales now come from organic traffic, which means more revenue without paying for every click.

02 · ai-enhanced microsite

shaping the future of wine.

we launched an AI-enhanced microsite built to surface on Google and within AI-powered chat discovery, fast. It complemented the main website, with a stronger focus on sales-driven conversion paths and newsletter growth.

ChatGPT began sending traffic to the microsite within six weeks of launch. The microsite delivered 97% engagement and grew subscribers who converted at an 8% rate.

it also helped sustain a 158% increase in subscriber-driven revenue over six months, by adding a new call-to-action-led channel in December, a month that is traditionally slower.

the microsite amplified results rather than acting alone. Social campaigns and the main website ran alongside it, and also contributed to Mailchimp performance.
