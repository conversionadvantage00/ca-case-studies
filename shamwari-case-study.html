thechallenge

safaris are expensive and complex. very few people click “book now” on a first visit, and Shamwari wanted to rely less on online travel agents and paid platforms for its bookings.

Shamwari needed three things at once: more qualified traffic from people already searching for a safari, a way to start a real conversation with travellers who weren’t ready to book yet, and a website that earned visits on its own through search.

so we gave each channel a clear job. Google Ads captured intent, Meta lead ads opened conversations, and SEO content built a steady source of organic enquiries.

thesearch

01/ 03google ads · 6 months vs the previous 6 months

thesocial

02/ 03meta lead generation · 6 months

increase direct bookings from social.

a cold “book now” button rarely works for a safari. lead generation ads turn anonymous clicks into one-on-one conversations with a reservations consultant, which close at a much higher rate.

what we did

  • ran two creative routes on Facebook: special offers for South African travellers, and emotional appeal ads built around escape and adventure
  • used instant lead forms that collect an email or WhatsApp number, plus intent signals such as travel month, party size and budget
  • passed every lead straight to Shamwari’s reservations team for a personal follow-up
  • used the lead data to power remarketing, lookalike audiences and nurture flows, reducing reliance on online travel agents and paid algorithms
