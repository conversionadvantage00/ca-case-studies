the brief

put Mantis Collection in front of the travellers most likely to book a luxury eco-escape, wherever they are in the world.

Mantis Collection’s properties combine luxury with conservation and heritage. The aim was to build awareness and brand affinity with experience-seeking travellers, and to turn that attention into website visits, “book now” actions and revenue.

paid media let us target by interest, behaviour, location and intent, so the message reached the people most likely to engage and convert.
